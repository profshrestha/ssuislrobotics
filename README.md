# Intelligent Systems Lab, Sonoma State University

Robotics and applied machine learning, in Salazar 2008 and 2007.

The lab runs ten GPU workstations, five Trossen WidowX AI six-axis manipulators, a set of
Interbotix RX150 arms, and depth cameras from Intel and Luxonis. The facilities support the
robotics course sequence, the lab's research in sim-to-real transfer, teleoperation and imitation
learning, and collaborative work with people outside both.

**Director:** Dr. Sudhir Shrestha, Department of Engineering Science.

## Getting an account

Accounts on the lab machines are issued to course students, lab members, and collaborators, and
are valid for one semester. Start with [Instructions for new users](new-users.md), which covers
the account itself, the first login, and how files are handled.

Requests for access go to the lab director.

## Using the lab

The machines are shared and are maintained remotely. Three points apply to everyone, and the
reasons are given in the pages above:

- **Log out at the end of a session and leave the machine powered on.** Logging out ends your
  session and protects your files. A machine that has been powered off cannot be reached for
  maintenance and is found inoperable by the next person.
- **Keep your work in version control or on a USB drive.** Accounts are local to each machine,
  so files do not follow you from one station to another.
- **Back up anything you want to keep before the semester ends.** Accounts and their contents are
  removed at the conclusion of each semester.
